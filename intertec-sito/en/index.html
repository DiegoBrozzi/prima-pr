Ghost Film · Smart glass

One click.
And the room disappears.

Ghost Film is a smart film applied to your existing glass. With the power off it diffuses light and turns opaque; switched on, it becomes perfectly clear again. Ideal for meeting rooms, medical practices, bathrooms and shop windows.

  • Controlled by switch, remote or home automation

FAQ

Window films:
quick answers.

The questions we hear most often before a site survey. If yours isn't here, just ask.

Can the films be applied to my existing windows?

Yes. Our films are applied directly to existing glass, with no need to replace or remove the window frames. Ghost Film is also installed on the glass already in place.

How much can I save with a solar control film?

Solar control films can cut air-conditioning costs by up to 50%. Actual savings depend on orientation, glass type and the film chosen; we assess them during the site survey.

Do the films protect against UV rays?

Yes. Anti-fading films filter the ultraviolet rays that fade artworks, furniture, fabrics and shop-window goods. That is why museums such as the Vatican Museums, the Royal Museums of Turin and GAM Milan chose them.

How does Ghost Film work?

Ghost Film is a smart film based on PDLC technology. With the power off it diffuses light and makes the glass opaque; when powered it turns clear again. It can be controlled by a switch, a remote or a home automation system.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Perugia. We carry out site surveys in Perugia, Terni and across Umbria, and we deliver projects throughout Italy.

Are the films guaranteed?

Yes. Throughout the warranty period we provide ongoing support and replace the film if it is damaged or turns out to be unsuitable.
